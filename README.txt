# PFR — [Processed Food Reward]

## Contact
Alexandra G. DiFeliceantonio — Dife@vt.edu
Zach Hutelin - Hutelin@vt.edu

## Study Design
- **Task**: Food auction task (4 runs: auction1–auction4)
- **Scanner**: Siemens Prisma 3T, Virginia Tech Carilion Research Institute
- **Field map**: AP/PA EPI fieldmaps acquired for each session
- **Participants**: N = 57

## Description
Brief description of the study: Participants completed 4 runs of a Becker-deGroot-Marschak auction task to assess food value while undergoing fMRI scanning. During each run, participants bid their willingness to pay between 0 and 5 USD on 28 different foods (14 UPF and 14 non-UPF) presented in a randomized order. Bids were placed on a visual analog scale with labels for $0, $2.50, and $5 USD, the cursor started in the middle at $2.50 on each trial. A modified version of the participant instructions used by Plassmann were implemented to inform participants of the task. Participants were instructed that they will see a series of food items and place a bid between $0 and $5 dollars for each item. At the end of the session, a food from one trial will be chosen at random and if they have bid higher than a randomly computer-generated price, they obtain and can consume the selected food, but the computer-generated price will be deducted from their session compensation. However, if they lose to the computer, they will be expected to wait in a testing room with no food for 30 minutes. 


## Data Acquisition
- MRI system: Siemens Prisma 3T
- Functional: multiband EPI, TR = 1.5 s, TE = 34 ms
- Slice thickness: 2 mm
- Field maps: AP and PA EPI (for susceptibility distortion correction)
